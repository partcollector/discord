Intro

Meet the New Age of Trading

Welcome to the ultimate Discord-powered platform for buying and selling genuine parts across the globe. Whether you're an individual with spare components or a business ready to scale internationally, our server connects sellers and buyers in real time across auto, moto, marine, truck, and industrial sectors.

With a sleek interface, verified transactions, and a truly global reach, Part Collector turns Discord into a trusted hub for parts discovery, listing, and distribution.

Part Collector is the first dedicated Discord marketplace built specifically for trading power and vehicle parts, giving enthusiasts and professionals a modern alternative to traditional listing platforms.

List and sell your parts with ZERO selling fees and ZERO insertion fees, so you keep more of every successful deal.

Our subscription plans are among the most affordable in the market, making it easy for both private sellers and businesses to scale their listings without breaking their budget.

Security

Your Safety Comes First

The main priority of our platform is Safety. Our Discord server operates under the latest official Discord Community Guidelines, using aggressive anti-spam and anti-scam policies to keep every interaction clean and secure.

Multiple layers of automated and manual protection work together to protect your listings, your deals, and your community experience 24/7.

Active Security Layers

  • Raid Alerts Protection
  • Strong Captcha Verification
  • Direct Message & Spam Protection
  • Automod Full Block Protection
  • 2FA Authenticator for stronger account security
  • Activity Status & Joining visibility set to OFF for extra privacy

All transactions are processed securely through PayPal adding an extra layer of buyer and seller protection.
