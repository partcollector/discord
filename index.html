Intro

Meet the New Age of Trading

Welcome to the ultimate Discord-powered platform for buying and selling genuine parts across the globe. Whether you're an individual with spare components or a business ready to scale internationally, our server connects sellers and buyers in real time across all power parts sectors.

With a sleek interface, verified transactions, and a truly global reach, Part Collector turns Discord into a trusted hub for parts discovery, listing, and distribution.

Part Collector is the first dedicated Discord marketplace built specifically for trading power and vehicle parts, giving enthusiasts and professionals a modern alternative to traditional listing platforms.

List and sell your parts with ZERO selling fees, so you keep more of every successful deal.

Our subscription plans are among the most affordable in the market, making it easy for both private sellers and businesses to scale their listings without breaking their budget.

Services

What We Offer

Part Collector is a complete, end-to-end spare parts trading ecosystem built on Discord. We handle everything from listing and discovery to payments, shipping, and tax compliance.

📋 Smart Listings

Create detailed listings with photos, vehicle compatibility, condition reports, and pricing. Our platform automatically organises and indexes your inventory for maximum discoverability.

🔍 Advanced Search & Discovery

Buyers can search across thousands of listings by brand, model, part type, category, and location. Our smart filtering helps find exactly what you need — fast.

💳 PayPal Payment Processing

All transactions are processed through PayPal for buyer and seller protection. Direct seller-to-buyer payments mean we never hold your funds.

🌍 Global Tax Compliance

Automated VAT/GST calculation for 201 countries. Support for OSS, IOSS, UK VAT, VOEC, Canadian GST/HST, Australian GST, and more. Provincial/state tax handling for US, Canada, and Brazil.

📦 Shipping Integration

Set up shipping rates per country or region. Real-time tracking link provided. Freight quotes available for oversized items.

🛡️ Dispute Guidance

We provide clear guidelines for dispute resolution between buyers and sellers. Our support team assists both parties in reaching a fair outcome through transparent communication.

🤖 24/7 Automated Support

Our bots handle everything — listing management, invoicing, shipping labels, order tracking, and DM notifications. Open a ticket anytime for personalised help.

Global Coverage

Security

Your Safety Comes First

The main priority of our platform is Safety. Our Discord server operates under the latest official Discord Community Guidelines, using aggressive anti-spam and anti-scam policies to keep every interaction clean and secure.

Multiple layers of automated and manual protection work together to protect your listings, your deals, and your community experience 24/7.

Active Security Layers

  • Raid Alerts Protection
  • Strong Captcha Verification
  • Direct Message & Spam Protection
  • Automod Full Block Protection
  • 2FA Authenticator for stronger account security
  • Activity Status & Joining visibility set to OFF for extra privacy
