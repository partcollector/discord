4. How We Use Your Data

  • To verify that your PayPal email address is valid and belongs to you (OTP verification process);
  • To create PayPal payment links for your subscription billing (where Part Collector is the merchant and receives the tier fee via PayPal);
  • To create PayPal checkout orders for purchases from sellers, using the seller’s verified PayPal email as the payee so the buyer pays the seller directly via PayPal. Funds go directly to the seller’s PayPal account and never pass through Part Collector; Part Collector takes no fee on these orders;
  • To automatically calculate and remove VAT from invoices for EU VAT-registered sellers (via VIES);
  • To display and manage your seller shipping profile, rates, and active listings;
  • To maintain subscription and payment records as required by Bulgarian accounting legislation;
  • To enforce Community rules, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse;
  • To respond to your support requests and legal rights enquiries;
  • For buyers who complete a payment: to link the PayPal email address from the payment confirmation to your Community Terms acceptance record, solely for the purpose of dispute resolution and enforcement of the Terms of Service you accepted.
  • To comply with EU DAC7 reporting obligations (Directive 2021/514) by collecting, verifying, and annually reporting seller identity information, tax identification numbers, financial identifiers, and quarterly transaction data to the Bulgarian National Revenue Agency.

5. Data Sharing

We share personal data only as strictly necessary:

  • PayPal: We use PayPal for two distinct purposes. (1) Subscriptions: we use the PayPal Subscriptions/Billing API to collect tier fees — here Part Collector is the merchant and receives the payment. (2) Orders: we use the PayPal Orders API to create checkout orders for purchases from sellers, using the seller’s verified PayPal email as the payee — the buyer pays the seller directly, funds go straight to the seller’s PayPal account, and Part Collector neither receives nor touches those funds and charges no fee. Payment capture and processing in both cases is governed entirely by PayPal’s own Privacy Policy, and PayPal acts as an independent data controller;
  • VIES (EU VAT Information Exchange System): EU sellers’ VAT numbers are submitted to the VIES system in real-time for validation. No data is retained from this query beyond what the seller has provided to us;
  • Brevo: We use Brevo to send OTP verification emails to sellers during PayPal email setup. See Brevo’s Privacy Policy;
  • OCI (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure): All Tool data is hosted on OCI servers in Turin, Italy (EU). This includes our archive storage (pc-archive, encrypted at rest) which holds order conversation transcripts, tier invoices and DAC7 reports. Oracle acts as our processor under the Oracle Services Data Processing Agreement incorporated into the Oracle Cloud Services Agreement. See Oracle’s Privacy Policy;
  • Google (Workspace): Our business mailbox info@partcollector.org runs on Google Workspace, so e-mail you send us (including contact-form messages) is processed by Google as our processor under the Google Cloud Data Processing Addendum. See Google’s Privacy Policy;
  • Carrier integrations (Sendcloud, Shippo, EasyPost): A paid seller may connect their own carrier account to print shipping labels. The delivery name and address are then transmitted to the carrier the seller uses, under that carrier’s own terms; the API credentials stay encrypted in our database. If a seller never connects a carrier account, no data is sent to these providers;
  • GitHub: Used for our source code and for a rolling off-site technical backup (application code and database backups). Archive copies of order-chat transcripts, invoices and DAC7 reports have been moved to OCI, and the database backup no longer contains order-chat messages. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement;
  • Cloudflare: Our website (partcollector.com) is served via Cloudflare for security and CDN. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy;
  • 17TRACK (17track.net): When a seller provides a tracking number for an order, a link to 17TRACK’s public tracking page is automatically generated so the buyer can monitor delivery. Clicking this link takes you to 17TRACK’s website, which is governed by their own Privacy Policy and Terms. No personal data is transmitted to 17TRACK by us — the tracking number is embedded in the URL, and any data sent to 17TRACK occurs only when the buyer voluntarily clicks the link.
  • Sellers (order fulfilment): When you place an order, your delivery name and address are shared with the seller solely so they can ship the item. Sellers may use this data only to fulfil the order and must not use it for any other purpose.
  • Bulgarian National Revenue Agency (NRA): Under EU DAC7 (Directive 2021/514), we are legally required to report EU-resident seller identity, tax identification numbers, address, financial identifiers, and quarterly transaction data annually to the Bulgarian NRA. Reports are submitted by 31 January following the end of each reporting year.
  • Other tax authorities (automatic exchange of information): In line with the OECD Model Rules for Reporting by Platform Operators and analogous national implementations (e.g. HMRC, the Norwegian tax administration, the Australian Taxation Office, the Canada Revenue Agency, New Zealand Inland Revenue and other authorities), seller data collected during account setup may be reported to the tax authority of the seller's country of residence — directly where we are registered, or through automatic exchange of information via the Bulgarian NRA.
  • Legal Authorities: Where we are legally obliged to disclose data (e.g., court orders, government requests).

Processors and sub-processors. The providers that process personal data on our instructions and under a data-processing agreement are: Oracle (OCI hosting + archive storage), Cloudflare (website delivery/security), Brevo (verification e-mail), Google (Workspace mailbox) and — only where a seller connects one — that seller’s chosen carrier integration. A current list of sub-processors can be requested at any time via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy).

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, use it for advertising profiling, or share it with any other party.

All personal data collected by Part Collector is stored on servers located within the European Economic Area (OCI Turin, Italy). For our own processing purposes we do not transfer your personal data outside the EEA, with two limited exceptions that we disclose here: (a) our business e-mail mailbox is provided by Google Workspace and (b) a rolling technical backup of our code and database is mirrored to GitHub, Inc. (USA) — both under the standard contractual clauses of their data-processing terms. Personal-data archives (chat transcripts, invoices, DAC7 reports) are deliberately kept in the EEA on OCI. Transfers required by tax reporting are described below.

International transfers for tax reporting. Where we are legally required to do so (EU DAC7 Directive 2021/514 and the OECD platform-reporting framework / national laws implementing it), seller tax-identification and identity data may be transferred to the tax authority of the seller's country of residence, including authorities located outside the EEA. Such transfers are made only under a legal obligation or for the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims (GDPR Art. 49(1)(e) / 49(1)(d)) and are limited to what the applicable reporting rules require.

PayPal, Brevo, and VIES operate as independent data controllers under their own privacy policies; please refer to those policies for information about their own data storage and international transfer practices.

8. Your Rights

Under EU GDPR you have the following rights:

Right of Access (Art. 15)

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

Sellers: Use “Delete My Account” (⚙️ Setup Shipping) or “Request Deletion” (⚖️ Legal & Privacy) in the Discord bot for immediate self-service deletion of all non-DAC7 profile, listing and PayPal data, and of your own conversation messages (subject to the 180-day PayPal dispute window). Buyers: Submit a written request via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy).

Important limitation: Under EU DAC7 regulations (Directive 2021/514), certain seller data (identity, TIN, date of birth, address, financial identifiers, and transaction records) must be retained for 10 years from the end of the relevant reporting year. This data cannot be deleted before that period expires, even if you delete your account. Other non-DAC7 data (listings, shipping profiles, etc.) is deleted immediately upon account deletion. Terms of Service consent records may be retained for up to 5 years under Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR where necessary to establish or defend legal claims, regardless of an erasure request.

Right to Restriction (Art. 18)

Request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances.

Right to Portability (Art. 20)

Receive your listing and seller data in a machine-readable format. Use the Export feature in the bot or contact us.

Right to Object (Art. 21)

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))

Withdraw any consent you have given at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

Right to Complain

Lodge a complaint with the CPDP (Bulgaria) or your national supervisory authority (EU).

To exercise any of the above rights, use the self-service deletion in the Discord bot (“Delete My Account” under Setup Shipping, or “Request Deletion” under Legal & Privacy), or submit a request via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy). We will respond within 30 days.
