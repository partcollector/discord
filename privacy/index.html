4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create PayPal payment links for your subscription billing (where Part Collector is the merchant and receives the tier fee via PayPal);
  • To create PayPal checkout orders for purchases from sellers, using the seller’s verified PayPal email as the payee so the buyer pays the seller directly via PayPal. Funds go directly to the seller’s PayPal account; Orders carry no Community fee;
  • To automatically calculate the tax on a sale from the declarations the seller has made and the figures the seller accepted or entered — EU VAT, EU OSS, IOSS, UK VAT, Norway VOEC, GST and the rest;
  • To remove or apply VAT on invoices for VAT-registered business sellers and buyers, including B2B reverse-charge exemptions;
  • To keep a record of how the tax on each order was calculated, so the order, the invoice and any question about it can be answered. It is not a statutory tax archive and is not reported to any tax authority;
  • To display and manage your seller shipping profile, rates, tax scheme settings, and active listings;
  • To keep your subscription and payment records while your account exists (the payment itself is handled by PayPal, and the tier subscription is billed by Discord);
  • To enforce Community rules, resolve disputes, and prevent abuse;
  • To respond to your support requests and legal rights enquiries;
  • For buyers who complete a payment: to link the PayPal email address from the payment confirmation to your Community Terms acceptance record, solely for the purpose of dispute resolution and enforcement of the Terms of Service you accepted.

5. Data Sharing

We share personal data only as strictly necessary:

  • PayPal: Payment for an order is made by the buyer to the seller through the PayPal invoice link we send, so PayPal handles the payment and its own processing. Tier purchases are billed by Discord Premium Apps, so no payment for a tier reaches us. Payment handling in both cases is governed by PayPal’s and Discord’s own Privacy Policy;
  • ECB (European Central Bank): We fetch daily exchange rates from the ECB for currency conversion. No personal data is transmitted;
  • OCI (Oracle Cloud Infrastructure): All Tool data is hosted on OCI servers in Turin, Italy (EU). See Oracle’s Privacy Policy;
  • Cloudflare: Our website (partcollector.com) is served via Cloudflare for security and CDN. See Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy;
  • Legal Authorities: Where we are legally obliged to disclose data (e.g., court orders, government requests).

Your personal data is used only to operate the service and shared only as listed in this section.

6. Data Storage & Location

PayPal operates as an independent data controller under its own privacy policy; please refer to it for information about its own data storage and international transfer practices.

8. Your Rights

Under EU GDPR you have the following rights:

Right of Access (Art. 15)

Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Right to Rectification (Art. 16)

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.

Right to Erasure (Art. 17)

Sellers: Use “Delete Account” in the Discord bot for immediate self-service deletion of all profile, listing, and PayPal data. Buyers: Submit a written request via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy). Note: the Terms of Service consent records may be kept beyond the erasure request where necessary to establish, exercise or defend a legal claim (Art. 17(3)(e) GDPR). That is the complete retention.

Right to Restriction (Art. 18)

Request that we restrict processing in certain circumstances.

Right to Portability (Art. 20)

Receive your listing and seller data in a machine-readable format. Use the Export feature in the bot or contact us.

Right to Object (Art. 21)

Object to processing based on legitimate interests.

Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3))

Withdraw any consent you have given at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

Right to Complain

Lodge a complaint with the CPDP (Bulgaria) or your national supervisory authority (EU).

To exercise any of the above rights, use the self-service “Delete Account” feature in the Discord bot, or submit a request via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy). We will respond within 30 days.
