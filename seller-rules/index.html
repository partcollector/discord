Seller Rules

Last updated: July 2026  ·  Part Collector Ltd

These rules are mandatory. By activating your seller account you agree to follow these operational rules at all times. Violations may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the Community.

1. Listing Standards

1

Accurate Descriptions. Every listing must truthfully describe the item — including condition (New / Used / Refurbished / For Parts), brand, OEM part number where available, compatible makes/models, and any faults, scratches, missing components, or prior repairs.

2

Real Photographs. You must upload clear photographs of the actual item being sold — not stock images or images from other listings. Photos must show the item's true condition, including any visible defects.

3

No Duplicate Listings. Each individual item may only be listed once. Creating multiple listings for the same item to gain visibility is prohibited. Bulk lots must be listed as a single listing with full content description.

4

Correct Category. You must list items in the correct category. Mis-categorised listings may be removed without notice.

5

Pricing Transparency. The listed price must be the full price for the item. Any shipping cost must be declared before the buyer confirms the order. Hidden fees or surprise charges after sale are strictly prohibited.

2. Order Fulfilment

6

Confirm within 24 hours. You must confirm or decline a paid order within 24 hours. Silent non-response will be treated as a failure to fulfil and may trigger an automatic refund to the buyer.

7

Dispatch within handling time. Ship the item within the handling time stated in your listing. If you cannot dispatch within that time due to an unforeseen circumstance, you must notify the buyer immediately via the bot and agree a revised timeline.

8

Tracking required on shipments over £/€20. For any order with a total value exceeding £20 (or €23 equivalent), you must use a tracked shipping service and provide the tracking number to the buyer upon dispatch.

9

Safe packaging. Items must be packaged appropriately to prevent damage in transit. Fragile parts must be bubble-wrapped and boxed. Damage caused by inadequate packaging is your responsibility and may result in a refund obligation.

10

Don't cancel without reason. Repeatedly cancelling confirmed orders is prohibited. If you can no longer fulfil an order, contact us immediately via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy).

3. Seller Type & VAT Compliance

11

Accurate seller type declaration. You must declare whether you are a Company or Private individual during setup. This classification determines how VAT is calculated on your sales. Providing false information may result in account suspension.

12

VAT validation. If you register as a company seller, your tax registration number will be validated against the relevant government authority: VIES (EU), HMRC (UK), Brreg (Norway), ABR (Australia), or NZBN API (New Zealand). You must provide accurate information.

13

Tax scheme declarations. Where offered, you may declare eligibility for tax schemes such as OSS, IOSS, UK VAT, or VOEC. These declarations affect the VAT charged to buyers at checkout. You are responsible for the accuracy of your declarations.

4. Communication Standards

14

Respond promptly. Buyers may message you through the bot regarding active orders. You must respond to order-related queries within 48 hours.

15

Respectful conduct. You must treat all buyers and support staff with courtesy and respect. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, or abusive conduct of any kind will result in immediate suspension.

16

No off-community transactions. You must not solicit or accept payments outside of the Part Collector payment system for sales that originated on the Community. This bypasses buyer protections and is a serious violation.

5. Prohibited Items

The following items are strictly forbidden on the Community:

  • Counterfeit, copied, or fake parts (e.g., non-genuine parts falsely marketed as OEM);
  • Stolen vehicles, components, or items with defaced/removed identification numbers;
  • Airbags, seatbelts, or safety-critical components that are expired, deployed, or beyond service life;
  • Items subject to a safety recall without disclosure and remediation evidence;
  • Items prohibited for sale in the EU or in the buyer's jurisdiction;
  • Non-automotive items (unless category-approved by a community admin);
  • Complete vehicles (whole cars, motorcycles, and other motor vehicles) — the Community is for parts and accessories only;
  • Dangerous or hazardous materials not properly classified.

Violating this section will result in immediate listing removal and account suspension or ban.

6. Feedback and Ratings

17

No feedback manipulation. Attempting to incentivise positive feedback, threatening buyers for negative feedback, or using secondary accounts to inflate your rating is a permanent ban offence.

18

Respond to negative feedback professionally. You may respond to buyer feedback through the community. Responses must be factual and professional. Retaliatory feedback or personal attacks are not permitted.

7. Account Standards

19

One seller account per person. Each individual may hold only one seller account. Operating multiple accounts to evade penalties or bans is a permanent ban offence.

20

Keep your PayPal connected. Your verified PayPal account must remain connected to your Discord profile at all times. Disconnecting your PayPal while having active listings or open orders is not permitted.

PayPal protection & payment holds. Payment processing is provided by PayPal under its own terms. PayPal's Seller Protection programme has eligibility limits that depend on the seller's account registration country and the payment method used (for example, accounts registered in Bulgaria are not eligible for Seller Protection for Guest Checkout / Standard Card payments). PayPal may also place holds, reserves or payment reviews on funds in accordance with its policies. These are PayPal decisions outside Part Collector's control, and the Community's own dispute and return process does not override them. Sellers are responsible for meeting PayPal's requirements to qualify for its protection.
PayPal disputes are filed directly with PayPal. Part Collector does not open, escalate, or decide PayPal disputes, and cannot intervene in them. Buyers and sellers file and manage payment disputes themselves through PayPal’s Resolution Center. Part Collector only mediates listing-quality disputes within the Community.
PayPal Buyer Protection limits. PayPal’s Buyer Protection does not cover items intended for resale (including multi-item or bulk transactions) and does not cover vehicles. Buyers purchasing parts for resale may not be protected by PayPal. Sellers remain responsible for fulfilling orders in accordance with these rules regardless of PayPal protection status.

8. Enforcement & Strike System

We operate a proportionate enforcement system. Actions are taken based on severity and frequency of violations:

Level Trigger Consequence
Warning First minor infraction (e.g., late dispatch, minor listing inaccuracy) Written notice; no suspension
Suspension Repeated minor violations; first moderate violation; failure to fulfil orders; providing false seller type or VAT information Temporary account suspension (7–30 days)
Permanent Ban Fraud, counterfeit listings, feedback manipulation, off-community payment solicitation, abuse, multiple account evasion Permanent removal; all listings deleted; pending transactions refunded

You may appeal any enforcement action via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy) within 14 days of the action. Appeals are reviewed by a moderator not involved in the original decision.

9. Compliance with Law

You are responsible for complying with all laws applicable to your selling activity, including but not limited to:

  • Consumer protection laws in the buyer's country (particularly EU consumer rights on distance selling);
  • VAT and tax obligations in your country, including accurate declaration of seller type and registration for applicable tax schemes (OSS, IOSS, UK VAT, VOEC);
  • Product safety legislation (e.g., GPSD / EU GPSR);
  • Export control and sanctions regulations.

Part Collector Ltd does not provide legal or tax advice. If you are unsure of your obligations, you should seek independent legal counsel.

10. Amendments

We may update these Seller Rules with 14 days' notice via the Discord server. Continued use of your seller account after that date constitutes acceptance of the updated rules.

11. Contact

Questions about these rules should be submitted via our contact form (select Legal & Privacy) or the #seller-support channel in our Discord server.
