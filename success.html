Before You Join

1Get Discord — already have it? Skip to step 2

Discord is a free app available on every platform. If you don't have it yet, download it from the official Discord website below.

Windows macOS iOS Android Linux Web Browser

Already installed? No need to re-download — continue to step 2.

2Link PayPal to your Discord account

Why do we ask for PayPal?
Our platform processes all payments through PayPal for buyer & seller protection. Linking it to your Discord account proves you have an active PayPal — no money is taken, no data is stored by us. This is a standard Discord account connection, exactly like linking Spotify or Steam.
  • Open the Discord Desktop App on your computer
  • Click the ⚙ gear icon (User Settings) at the bottom left, next to your username
  • In the left sidebar scroll down and click Connections
  • Find and click PayPal a browser window opens asking you to log in to PayPal
  • Log in and you will be redirected back to Discord
  • Done — you should now see PayPal listed under your Connections
  • Open the Discord Mobile App on your phone
  • Tap your profile picture at the bottom right
  • Tap the ⚙ gear icon at the top right to open Settings
  • Scroll down and tap Connections
  • Tap Add (top right corner)
  • Find and click PayPal a browser window opens asking you to log in to PayPal
  • Log in and you will be redirected back to Discord
  • Done — you should now see PayPal listed under your Connections
  • Go to discord.com in your browser and log in
  • Click the ⚙ gear icon (User Settings) at the bottom left
  • In the left sidebar click Connections
  • Find and click PayPal your browser will open the PayPal login page
  • Log in and you will be redirected back to Discord
  • Done — you should now see PayPal listed under your Connections

3Join the server

Once PayPal is linked, click the button below. Discord will ask you to authorise our app — this grants us permission to verify your connections and add you to the server automatically. No password or payment information is ever shared with us.

Ready? Your PayPal is linked — let's go.
